Privacy policy

rclone Drive · last updated 8 November 2026

rclone Drive is a personal Google API project used by one person, its owner, to reach their own Google Drive from their own computer through rclone.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in, Google grants rclone access to that account's Google Drive files. rclone reads and writes those files only so they appear as a folder on the owner's computer.

What happens to the data

  • Files travel directly between Google and the owner's computer.
  • No data is sent to, collected by, or stored on any other server.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • The sign-in token is stored only in rclone's configuration file on the owner's computer.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account under Security, then Your connections to third-party apps and services.

Contact

Reach the owner through their GitHub profile.
